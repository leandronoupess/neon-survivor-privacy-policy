Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

How the app works

Neon Survivor works offline and does not collect, transmit, share, or sell personal data. The Android version uses the VIBRATE permission only for haptic feedback. It does not require Internet access or use a publisher-operated network service.

Data stored on your device

Progress, settings, and high scores are stored only on your device. The publisher cannot access this data. You can remove it by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling the app.

Accounts and third-party services

The game has no user accounts, advertising, in-app purchases, analytics, tracking tools, advertising identifier, or publisher-operated online services.

Changes and contact

If a future version processes data, this policy and the Data safety declarations will be updated before that version is released.
